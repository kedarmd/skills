# Inspiration research and synthesis

**Use when:** researching or adapting visual references. Defining behavior: translate principles into product-specific choices.

**Inputs:** references, product goals/users/platform, existing contract, research capabilities, and requested research or implementation scope.

1. DISCOVER the product and reference access. Read [reference synthesis](../protocol/inspiration.md) for observation, explanation, research, separation, synthesis and similarity review.
2. CONTRACT: identify approved rules the direction must preserve. Uncertain interpretations stay labeled; inaccessible sources stay unavailable.
3. PLAN alternatives by connecting reference observations to product needs. State why each adaptation serves the product and what is intentionally different.
4. For research-only work, present sourced alternatives and tradeoffs without editing UI. For implementation, continue through [new-screen](new-screen.md) or [refine-screen](refine-screen.md); canonical changes add [design-system change](design-system-change.md).
5. VERIFY source claims and inspect the resulting direction for unintended literal replication. Applied output also requires normal actual-result verification.

**Complete when:** the direction has traceable reasoning, product fit, and explicit limitations; any implemented screen has its own verification and acceptance status.

**Recovery:** if research access is absent, explain the evidence limit. Rework literal replication; route conflicts with approved foundations to CONTRACT rather than silently rewriting them.
